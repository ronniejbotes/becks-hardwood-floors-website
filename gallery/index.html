The floor is probably still in there

Gray, scratched, worn through along the walking routes — floors that look finished usually are not. Sanding takes off the failed finish and a hair of the wood, and what comes back is the same board that went down decades ago.

From bare subfloor to finished room

Once the carpet and tack strips are out, what is left is plywood, staples and adhesive. New boards go down row by row off a straight working line, and the room changes character entirely.
